# S14 evidence review — development atlas

- **Reviewed:** 2026-10-04
- **Role pass:** evidence-reviewer (same Arena coding agent as implementation; not an independent scientific review).
- **Purpose:** Verify the small set of source statements needed to label the atlas. This is a scoped narrative source check, not a systematic review of all cognition, health, emotion, social development, or the interventions proposed below.

## Source-to-copy register

Do not use it as a comprehensive verdict on current programs. |

## User-facing copy rules

- Say **“practice this task/strategy”** rather than “improve your brain” unless a separate, exact outcome has been independently established.
- Label task scores by the task and item set. No percentile, IQ/EQ, brain age, diagnosis, or generalized ability inference.
- Label sleep, activity, nutrition and social support as **foundations/context**, not as app-delivered cognitive training.
- Label emotions, resilience, communication, creativity and habits as **optional skills or reflection prompts**, not clinical tools or validated treatment.
- Use “may,” “in the studied contexts,” and named populations when needed. No causal language from association or a broad guideline.
- Do not cite a source at a card merely as an endorsement. Include the source's role and non-claim in the expandable source note and this register.

## Review limits and next work

The sources above were opened through the publishers/official WHO and NIA sites on 2026-10-04. This review did not perform a new systematic search, formal risk-of-bias appraisal, full evidence grading, or consultation with a clinician, dietitian, exercise professional, psychologist, developmental researcher, or lived-experience panel. The atlas should be treated as educational orientation and self-guided prompts only. Any future intervention-like pathway or audience-specific advice needs a new claim-by-claim review and appropriate subject-matter oversight.
